### Project Description
[An ecommerce Application for learning, creating , selling and buying all kinds of art easily ]

### The Problem statement
[Problem Statement for Snailtoon App

In today’s digital era, artists face multiple challenges in showcasing, selling, and monetizing their artwork. While social media platforms like Instagram and WhatsApp provide exposure, they lack integrated selling tools, making transactions complex and unreliable. Artists often struggle with inconsistent payments, difficulty in managing orders, and the absence of a dedicated space where art lovers can easily discover and purchase their work.Firslty to start in our area.]

### The Solution
[Snailtoon is an all-in-one platform that bridges the gap between social media selling and professional art marketplaces. It allows users to:
✅ Learn Art through structured free and affordable courses.
✅ Sell Art with an easy-to-use platform while avoiding the hassle of negotiating on Instagram and WhatsApp.
✅ Buy Unique Artwork from talented artists in a dedicated marketplace.
✅ Seamlessly Manage Transactions with transparent pricing, a secure payment gateway.
✅ Customize User Experience with profile management, notification settings, and multiple language options.]
